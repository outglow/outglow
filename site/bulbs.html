Supported bulbs

Products known to work with Outglow. Inside each is one of 21 module versions, called boards; Outglow has a firmware image for each.

Check the bulb, not the box. The same product has shipped with different modules, and a vendor update can change what a bulb reports. The flasher reads the board from the bulb itself and only installs the image made for it, so use this list to find candidates.

How sure is each match?

  • confirmed a bulb with that article number was seen reporting this board (in Zigbee2MQTT reports, its device database, or on a bulb we own).
  • likely the same product family and light as a confirmed one, but nobody has reported its board yet.
  • inferred only the board's name points to it (for example c5_E14: 5 W colour, E14 socket). Treat it as a guess until the flasher confirms it.
