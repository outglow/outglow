Install

You install Outglow once over Bluetooth, from your browser. After that, Zigbee2MQTT keeps it up to date.

What you need

  • A supported bulb still on its original firmware.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS) or on Android. Safari and every browser on iPhone and iPad cannot talk to Bluetooth devices.
  • Zigbee2MQTT. Other Zigbee hubs can control the bulb too, but the diagnostics and the automatic updates are made for Zigbee2MQTT.

1. Install over Bluetooth

  1. Switch the bulb off and on. For the next 20 minutes it accepts a Bluetooth connection (an update already running is not cut off when that time is up).
  2. Open the flasher on a device within a few metres of the bulb.
  3. Click Connect to a bulb and pick it from the list. The flasher reads its model and proposes the matching Outglow release.
  4. Tick the confirmation and click Start the update. It takes about four minutes; keep the page open and the device awake.
  5. When it is done, the bulb restarts on its own. If it is still dark after a minute, switch it off and on.

2. Add it to Zigbee2MQTT

  1. Click Permit join in Zigbee2MQTT. The bulb starts looking for a network as soon as it restarts.
  2. Not there after a few minutes? Reset it and permit joining again.
  3. The bulb shows up as open-eglo-dimm, open-eglo-tw or similar. If it was already in Zigbee2MQTT before, it is simply interviewed again.
  4. To see the diagnostics and the Bluetooth settings, add the external converter open-eglo.mjs.

Factory reset

A bulb has no button, so the reset is a pattern of switching it on and off at the wall: three short power-ons, two long ones, then on again. It works the same on the original firmware and on Outglow. It removes the bulb from its Zigbee network and turns Bluetooth back on.

  1. Switch on for about 1 second, then off. Do this three times.
  2. Switch on for about 12 seconds, then off. Do this twice.
  3. Switch on and leave it on. The bulb blinks three times when the reset worked.

Leave it off for about 6 seconds each time. A short power-on must stay under 6 seconds, a long one between 6 and 30. Got it wrong? Leave the bulb on for half a minute and start again.

3. Get updates

Add the Outglow update list to Zigbee2MQTT's configuration.yaml:

ota:
  zigbee_ota_override_index_location: https://outglow.github.io/outglow/ota/index.json

The bulb checks for a new version once a day. Zigbee2MQTT then marks the update as available and installs it when you click Update, or on a schedule or automation you set up. More about updates →

Going back to the original firmware

Use the same flasher: connect to the bulb and click Restore original firmware. It downloads the manufacturer's firmware for that bulb, checks it and puts it back, the same way Outglow went on.

If you switched Bluetooth off in Outglow, switch it back on in Zigbee2MQTT first. A reset turns it back on too, but also removes the bulb from your network.
