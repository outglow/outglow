3. Update

    Advanced options

    Only if an update is refused without it. A bulb paired to the vendor app has its own mesh name and password; a new bulb is "unpaired", with a password derived from its MAC (the first six bytes of its Zigbee IEEE address).

    Larger blocks need a larger ATT_MTU, which the browser negotiates on its own and does not report; the original firmware takes up to 240 B, Outglow up to 57 B. If the transfer fails, go back to 16 B.

    Another hardware revision may have its LEDs on other pins: the bulb can stay dark and only Zigbee OTA (if it joins at all) could fix it. Type I accept to go on.
