Outglow and the original firmware

What changes when you switch. Tap i on a row to see what it means.

Original firmwareOutglow
Works with the vendor appYesYes

Over Bluetooth, Outglow still looks like the original bulb, so the AwoX app keeps working: login, brightness, colour, colour animation, candle mode, and the status the app reads back.

Works with any Zigbee 3.0 hubYesYes

Both are standard Zigbee lights. In Zigbee2MQTT, Outglow works without a converter: it reports its own model, and Zigbee2MQTT builds the device from what the bulb shows. Interview the bulb again after switching.

Updates from your Zigbee hubNoYes

The original firmware has no Zigbee update support; the only way to update it is the vendor app, over Bluetooth, next to the bulb. With Outglow, new versions show up in Zigbee2MQTT like for any other device.

Switches off at the end of a fadeNoYes

A hub's “fade to off” dims to the lowest level and expects the light to switch off there, as the Zigbee Cluster Library says. The original firmware stays at its dimmest glow instead. Outglow follows revision 8 of the library and was checked against its text.

Recovers when a router disappearsPoorlyYes

Bulbs are the routers of your Zigbee network, so this matters for every device behind them. With both firmwares in one emulated network, an original bulb kept sending to a router that was gone for as long as we watched (four minutes), and after a short power cut it sometimes searched every channel and never came back. Outglow drops a router it no longer hears, finds a new route and rejoins after power cuts.

Network diagnostics in your hubNoYes

Outglow shows how many frames it relayed, retried and lost, its neighbours and children, why it last restarted, and how much radio time Bluetooth took: all 32 counters of the Zigbee Diagnostics cluster, plus a few of its own.

Bluetooth can be switched offNoYes, or only for minutes after power-on

The bulb has one radio. Time spent on Bluetooth is time it can't hear Zigbee. Outglow lets you keep Bluetooth on, switch it off, or keep it only for a few minutes after the bulb is powered on, from your hub. A reset turns it back on.

Source codeClosedOpen, GPL

If the original firmware has a bug, only the vendor can fix it. Outglow is free software: anyone can read it, run it in the emulator and fix it.

Going back–Any time

The flasher puts the original firmware back over Bluetooth, and your hub can do it over Zigbee. Outglow never touches the vendor's bootloader, so after going back the bulb is what it was before.

Network behaviour and join times were measured in the Outglow emulator, which runs the real firmware images, the original ones included, through the same scenarios. Outglow itself runs on a few of its developers' bulbs so far. How to install and how to go back →
