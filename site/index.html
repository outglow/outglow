Open firmware for your smart bulbs

Outglow is open-source firmware for EGLO connect.z and AwoX Zigbee bulbs. It makes them reliable routers for your Zigbee network and still works with the vendor's app. You install it from your browser in about five minutes.

How to install Supported bulbs Source code

What you get

  • A steadier Zigbee network: your bulbs pass messages along reliably.
  • Built to the Zigbee 3.0 standard, so it works with any Zigbee hub.
  • The vendor app keeps working, and you can always go back.
  • Don't need Bluetooth? Switch it off, and the Zigbee connection gets even steadier.
  • Detailed diagnostics: neighbours, signal quality, retries, a log of what happened, in your hub or in the console.
  • Updates over the air through Zigbee, like any other firmware update: no app, no cable.
  • Free and open source.

Which bulbs?

Zigbee bulbs and lights sold as EGLO connect.z and AwoX that use the Telink TLSR8258 module: filament bulbs, GU10 and E14 spots, colour bulbs and ceiling lights. Outglow has a firmware image for each of the 21 module versions inside them, and the flasher picks the right one.

How it gets onto a bulb

  1. The first time, over Bluetooth. Open the flasher in Chrome or Edge, pick the bulb, and it installs the right image, the same way the vendor app installs its updates.
  2. After that, over Zigbee. Your Zigbee hub offers new Outglow versions like any other firmware update.
  3. Changed your mind? The original firmware goes back the same way.
