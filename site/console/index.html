Before connecting

The console talks to one bulb directly over Bluetooth, the way the vendor app does. It shows what the bulb knows about itself and its Zigbee network, refreshing the main status about once a second and the other readings about every two seconds. It also lets you change light settings. The page communicates directly with the bulb; its readings are not sent to an Outglow server.

  • A bulb running Outglow with Bluetooth on. The original firmware doesn't have this service. If you limited Bluetooth to a few minutes after power-on, switch the bulb off and on first.
  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop or Android, within a few metres of the bulb.
  • Reading needs nothing else. To control the light or change settings, log in with the same network name and password as in the vendor app. An unpaired bulb normally uses the network name unpaired. Click Log in to try the displayed credentials. If that fails for an unpaired bulb and the browser exposes its Bluetooth address, the console also tries a password derived from that address.

Tap i next to a heading or a counter to see what it means.

Settings

These settings control how the light returns after a power cut and how it handles changes while off. They are stored in the bulb and survive power cuts.

Fixed power-on brightness uses the bulb’s scale: 1 is dimmest and 254 is brightest. A fixed white tone uses mireds, a colour-temperature unit where a larger value means warmer white.

Running

How long the bulb has been running and why it last started. A watchdog restart means the software did not respond to its safety timer in time, so the chip restarted it. The reset sequence shows the internal progress marker for the wall-switch reset pattern; it is not a count of power cycles. The install guide explains that pattern step by step.

Zigbee

The bulb's place in your Zigbee network. Neighbours are nearby routers it can hear. Having few neighbours can mean there are fewer alternate routes, but the count alone does not establish a fault. Connected end devices, often battery-powered sensors or remotes, use this bulb to hold and forward messages. Reception quality and signal strength describe the last received message; neither reading alone measures the reliability of the whole route.

Diagnostic readings

This is a subset of the diagnostic readings available over Zigbee: the standard counters and selected Outglow readings. Some are running totals; others describe the current state. A counter may return to zero when cleared or when it reaches its limit. The identifier column is the reading’s Zigbee lookup number, not a measurement. Open a row for its meaning and a guide to interpreting it.
