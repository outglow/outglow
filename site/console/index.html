What is this, and what do I need?

The console talks to one bulb directly over Bluetooth, the way the vendor app does. It shows what the bulb knows about itself and its Zigbee network, refreshed every second, and lets you control it. Nothing leaves your browser: there is no server between you and the bulb.

  • A bulb running Outglow with Bluetooth on. The original firmware doesn't have this service. If you limited Bluetooth to a few minutes after power-on, switch the bulb off and on first.
  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop or Android, within a few metres of the bulb.
  • Reading needs nothing else. To control the light or change settings, log in with the same network name and password as in the vendor app. A bulb that was never added to the app uses unpaired, and the console finds its password by itself.

Tap i next to a heading or a counter to see what it means.

Running

Zigbee

The bulb's place in your Zigbee network. Neighbours are the routers it hears (2–6 is typical at home; 0–1 means it's at the edge). Children are battery devices that joined through it. LQI (0–255) and RSSI (dBm) describe the last frame it received: higher is better.

Frames sent to one neighbour need an acknowledgement; without one the bulb sends again. Under 10 % retries is a good link, 15–30 % a busy channel, over 30 % a poor link. Failures, frames never acknowledged, should stay under 1 %.

All counters

Every value of the Zigbee Diagnostics cluster (0x0B05), the same numbers your hub can read, plus Outglow's own from 0x5000. Counters only grow, and they wrap around at their maximum. Tap a row for what it counts and what a healthy bulb shows.
